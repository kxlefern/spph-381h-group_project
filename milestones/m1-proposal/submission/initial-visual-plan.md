**Planned figure:** 

Stacked bar chart of food insecurity rate in Canada from 2018 to 2024 of the households of people of different age groups, with one panel for each age group (children under 18, adults 18-64, and seniors 65+). Each bar is split into marginal, moderate, and severe food insecurity, and the total rate of food insecurity will be labelled above each bar. All panels will share a y-axis so that the different age group panels can be directly compared. 

**Audience Purpose:** 

Help the audience see how the changes in food security status over the years varied by severity and total amount between age groups

**Misinterpretation Risk:** 

The graph could be interpreted as age having a causal link with food insecurity. The segments of each bar could be interpreted as shares of food-insecure people, when it is actually percentages of everyone in that age group, including those who are food secure. The chart may also be misinterpreted as representing the number of people who personally go hungry in each age group, when it actually reflects the food-insecurity status of the person’s household. The caption will say that the summary is from weighted national estimates from Statistics Canada’s Canadian Income Survey and that small year-to-year differences may reflect sampling error
