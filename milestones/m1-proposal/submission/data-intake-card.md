indigenous_data_relevance:  Indigenous identity is included as an examined characteristic.  The use of Indigenous identity as a demographic variable requires consideration of the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance and OCAP. Although this project uses publicly available, aggregated data, these frameworks emphasize the importance of Indigenous data sovereignty, ethical data use, and respect for Indigenous communities' rights to govern information about themselves. Findings will therefore be interpreted with care, avoiding deficit-based narratives and acknowledging the importance of Indigenous leadership and involvement in decisions regarding data interpretation and policy development.

Privacy and stewardship considerations: The dataset is publicly available through the Government of Canada's Open Data Portal and is published and maintained by Statistics Canada. The data originate from the Canadian Income Survey and are subject to confidentiality protections under the Statistics Act. Published results are aggregated, with disclosure control measures used to protect respondents' identities. As the project uses publicly available summary statistics rather than individual-level records, privacy risks are minimal. Data will be used in accordance with the Open Government Licence – Canada, with appropriate attribution to Statistics Canada.
