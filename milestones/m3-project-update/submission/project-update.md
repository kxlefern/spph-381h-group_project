**Current title:** Household food insecurity status across British Columbia, Quebec, and Ontario in 2024

**Audience:** Federal public health staff examining trends in household food security in British Columbia, Quebec, and Ontario in 2024

**Refined primary question:** How does household food insecurity status vary across British Columbia, Quebec, and Ontario in 2024?

**Data and sample:** Publicly available CSV dataset published by Statistics Canada through Government of Canada's Open Data Portal. We restricted the cohort to BC, Quebec, and Ontario only.

**Stable finding:** In 2024, around 4,254,000 are food secure. 644,800 are moderately food insecure. 317,500 are marginally food insecure. 319,300 are severely food insecure.
